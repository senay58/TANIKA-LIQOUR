# 🍷 Tanika Liquor Management System: Complete User Guide

Welcome to the **Tanika Liquor** official management portal. This system is designed to streamline inventory tracking, sales processing, and financial oversight with a premium, high-performance interface.

---

## 🏷️ Visual Identity & Branding
The **Tanika Logo** (the Wine icon 🍷) represents quality and precision. You will find it in the top-left corner of both the Admin and Sales portals.
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Use the Sun/Moon icon in the top right to switch between themes. The system looks best in **Dark Mode** for a premium "Lounge" aesthetic.

---

## 👥 User Roles & Access

### 1. Admin Portal
**Access**: Requires the Admin secret code or username/password.
- **Dashboard**: High-level overview of total sales, low-stock alerts, and daily revenue.
- **Inventory**: Full control to add, edit, or delete products.
- **Categories**: Organize your stock (Whiskey, Gin, Wine, etc.).
- **Finances**: View the Cash Ledger and manage Customer Credits.
- **Reports**: Generate professional PDF reports for your records.

### 2. Sales Portal
**Access**: Designed for staff members using sales credentials.
- **Sales Entry**: A fast, search-optimized grid for adding items to a cart.
- **Cart**: Handles bulk sales, customer info (Phone/Name), and payment methods (Cash or Bank).
- **Sales Detail**: Allows staff to see their own performance metrics and transaction history.

---

## 📦 Managing Your Inventory

### Adding Products
1. Navigate to **Inventory**.
2. Click **"Add Product"**.
3. Fill in the details. **Price In** is what you paid; **Price Out** is the selling price.
4. **Note**: When you add a new product with stock, the system automatically deducts the cost from your **Cash Ledger**.

### Categories
- Use the **Manage Categories** button to create new groupings. 
- You can assign unique emojis to each category to make the Sales Entry grid more visual.

---

## 💰 Finance & Sales Workflow

### Processing a Sale
1. In the **Sales Portal**, search for a product or filter by category.
2. Click the product to add it to the **Cart**.
3. Open the **Cart** (top right) to review.
4. Enter **Customer Info** (optional but recommended for Credits).
5. Choose **Cash** or **Bank**. If Bank, enter the Reference Number.
6. Click **"Complete Sale"**.

### Customer Credits (Debts)
- If a customer is buying on credit, toggle the **"Credit Sale"** option in the cart.
- Set a **Due Date**.
- Credits appear in the **Finances** tab for the Admin to track and mark as paid.

### The Cash Ledger
The **Finances** tab tracks every cent:
- **Green (+)**: Revenue from sales.
- **Red (-)**: Restocking costs (buying new liquor).
- **Blue (+)**: Credit payments received.

## 📊 Reports & Analytics
- **Inventory Report**: Export a full list of your stock valuation.
- **Sales Report**: Filter by date range to see exactly what sold and when.
- **Salesperson Performance**: Compare revenue between staff members to track efficiency.

---

## 🛡️ Security Best Practices
- **Auto-Logout**: You can set an inactivity timer in the Admin Settings to keep the system secure.
- **Audit Logs**: Every time a category is added or stock is changed, a record is saved for the Admin to review.
- **Credentials**: Change your passwords regularly in the **Settings** menu.
